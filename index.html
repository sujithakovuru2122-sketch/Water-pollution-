Water pollution happens when harmful substances such as waste, chemicals and plastic enter rivers, lakes, groundwater and oceans. It harms people, animals and nature. Explore the sections below to learn how it happens and how we can stop it.

About Water Pollution

Water pollution is the contamination of water bodies such as rivers, lakes, groundwater and oceans by harmful substances. These substances make water unsafe for drinking, farming, and for the plants and animals that live in it.

Less than 1% of Earth's water is easily available fresh water. When that small share is polluted, the cost falls on our health, our food and our environment.

Causes of Water Pollution

🏭

Industrial waste

Factories release chemicals, heavy metals and hot water into rivers, often without proper treatment.

🛍️

Plastic waste

Bottles and bags thrown into drains and rivers end up in the sea, where they break into tiny microplastics.

🚰

Sewage water

Untreated household sewage carries germs and nutrients that spread disease and choke water bodies.

🌾

Agricultural chemicals

Fertilisers and pesticides wash off fields with rain and seep into streams and groundwater.

🛢️

Oil spills

Oil from ships and pipelines floats on water, blocks oxygen and coats the animals it touches.

Types of Water Pollution

🏞️

Surface water pollution

Contamination of rivers, lakes and ponds that people use every day for drinking, washing and farming.

⛲

Groundwater pollution

Pollutants soak through soil into underground water, which is hard and slow to clean.

🐚

Marine pollution

Plastic, oil and waste carried by rivers and ships damage oceans and coral reefs.

⚗️

Chemical pollution

Toxic substances from industry, farms and homes dissolve in water and poison life in it.

Effects of Water Pollution

  • Human health problems. Dirty water spreads cholera, typhoid, diarrhoea and hepatitis, and long-term exposure to chemicals can damage organs.
  • Death of aquatic animals. Poison and low oxygen kill fish, turtles and other water life.
  • Environmental damage. Polluted water harms plants, soil and the whole food chain.
  • Shortage of clean drinking water. As more sources become unsafe, clean water gets scarcer and costlier.

Prevention of Water Pollution

🚯

Keep plastic out

Avoid throwing plastic into rivers. Use a bin, and carry a reusable bottle and bag.

♻️

Treat wastewater

Industries must treat their wastewater before releasing it.

🧪

Use fewer chemicals

Reduce chemical use in farming, gardens and cleaning.

🏞️

Clean lakes and rivers

Join clean-up drives to keep lakes and rivers clean.

📢

Spread awareness

Tell family, friends and neighbours what you learned here.
