<section id="about">
    <h2>About Water Pollution</h2>
    <p>
        Water pollution occurs when harmful substances such as
        chemicals, plastics, sewage and waste enter rivers,
        lakes, oceans and other water sources.
        It makes water unsafe for humans, animals and plants.
    </p>
    <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1530053969600-caed2596d242?w=1000"
         alt="Polluted water">
</section>

<section id="causes">
    <h2>Causes of Water Pollution</h2>
    <ul>
        <li>Industrial waste released into rivers.</li>
        <li>Plastic and garbage dumping.</li>
        <li>Untreated sewage water.</li>
        <li>Excessive use of fertilizers and pesticides.</li>
        <li>Oil spills and chemical leakage.</li>
    </ul>
</section>

<section id="effects">
    <h2>Effects of Water Pollution</h2>
    <ul>
        <li>Causes diseases in humans.</li>
        <li>Harms aquatic animals and plants.</li>
        <li>Reduces the availability of clean drinking water.</li>
        <li>Damages aquatic ecosystems.</li>
        <li>Disturbs the natural balance of the environment.</li>
    </ul>
</section>

<section id="prevention">
    <h2>Prevention of Water Pollution</h2>
    <ul>
        <li>Do not throw plastic into water bodies.</li>
        <li>Treat industrial waste before releasing it.</li>
        <li>Reduce the use of chemical fertilizers.</li>
        <li>Keep rivers, lakes and ponds clean.</li>
        <li>Spread awareness about water conservation.</li>
    </ul>
</section>
